1.pinMode(pin,STATUS);
Status-INPUT or OUTPUT

2. HOW TO GIVE A DIGITIAL SIGNAL
CODE: digitalWrite(pin,STATUS);
STATUS- HIGH or LOW

3. delay(ms)
to stop the program.

void setup()
{
  pinMode(4, OUTPUT);
   pinMode(2, OUTPUT);
}

void loop()
{
  
  digitalWrite(4,HIGH);
   digitalWrite(2,LOW);
  delay(1000);
  digitalWrite(4,LOW);
  digitalWrite(2,HIGH);
  delay(1000);
}

value - 0-255
